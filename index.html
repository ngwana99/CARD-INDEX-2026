What happens to this data

Entries are collected in one shared register for GHS Mbonjo Limbe. The Principal's office / HOD Computer Science compiles the register into the official spreadsheet format and forwards it, together with the required supporting photocopies (recruitment decision, latest pay voucher, reclassification decision where applicable, birth certificate, appointment decisions, resumption-of-duty certificate), to MINFOPRA South West ahead of the 31 July 2026 deadline. This tool only collects the data fields — physical/photocopy documents still travel through the school as usual.
